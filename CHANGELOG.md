# Changelog

[简体中文](CHANGELOG.zh-CN.md)

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-08

- Added `GlassCard`, `GlassCardMaterial`, `GlassSwatch`, and `GlassPaper` for macOS 14+.
- Added nine color presets, custom tint and opacity, configurable blur, and configurable card corner radius.
- Added a material study with color and blur controls.
- Added Layered and Ring demo layouts, click and drag expansion, optional hover expansion, and Reduce Motion support.
- Added four material tests, including three rendered-pixel checks and a six-state PNG export command.
- Added English and Simplified Chinese documentation, screenshots, and MIT license notices.

The material uses a supplied backdrop rather than automatic capture of live sibling views. The demo approximates overlapping layers; large-stack performance has not been benchmarked.
